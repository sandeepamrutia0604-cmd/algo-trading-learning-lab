    Changing a model affects newly generated days (Advance or Regenerate), not past candles. These settings apply to the simulated stocks (ALPHA, BETA, GAMMA, DELTA); imported real stocks replay their real history instead, one day per advance, and stop when it runs out (Reset replays it from the start).

    Changes what "Reset" restores and what P&L/return % are measured against. Applying it immediately resets the simulation (clears trades and positions) at the new amount -- useful once real, higher-priced stocks make ₹1,00,000 too little to trade comfortably.

    Off: strategies auto-trade a fixed quantity per signal, same as before. On: position size is computed from your risk per trade and stop-loss below, a stop-loss auto-exits an auto-trade strategy's position, and every BUY (manual or auto-trade) is capped by max open positions and max allocation per stock.

    Add a stock from a file you downloaded yourself -- no broker login needed. The first row must name the columns (Date, Open, High, Low, Close, Volume). Tabs or commas, dates like 01 Oct 2026 or 2026-10-01, and numbers like 1,180.1 all work, in any row order. By default the file is merged into the stock's existing history by date; tick Replace to discard the old history first. The stock starts with its first ~60 days showing and replays the rest as you advance the market. ALPHA, BETA, GAMMA and DELTA are simulated and can't be imported over.
