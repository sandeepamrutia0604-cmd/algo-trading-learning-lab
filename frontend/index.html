    Stock -

    Changing a model affects newly generated days (Advance or Regenerate), not past candles. These settings apply to the simulated stocks (ALPHA, BETA, GAMMA, DELTA and any practice stocks you create); imported real stocks replay their real history instead, one day per advance, and stop when it runs out (Reset replays it from the start).

    Makes up a company whose prices the simulator generates, like ALPHA, BETA, GAMMA and DELTA. It gets the same history as they do, up to today's market date, and then advances, regenerates and resets with them. Reset keeps the behaviour you chose here. Use it to test how a strategy copes with a market you design: a steady uptrend, a choppy sideways range, a wild swinger.

    Changes what "Reset" restores and what P&L/return % are measured against. Applying it immediately resets the simulation (clears trades and positions) at the new amount -- useful once real, higher-priced stocks make ₹1,00,000 too little to trade comfortably.

    Off: strategies auto-trade a fixed quantity per signal, same as before. On: position size is computed from your risk per trade and stop-loss below, a stop-loss auto-exits an auto-trade strategy's position, and every BUY (manual or auto-trade) is capped by max open positions and max allocation per stock.

    Off: orders fill at the quoted price and cost nothing. On: every paper trade, auto-trade and backtest pays slippage (a BUY fills a little above the quote, a SELL a little below) plus brokerage and taxes, so results look like what a real account would have kept. Realized P&L is net of all of it.

    The defaults are typical of a discount broker (0.03% brokerage capped at ₹20 an order) with taxes and levies approximated as one flat percentage -- an educational estimate, not an exact statement of STT, GST or stamp duty. Check your own broker's charges for real numbers.

    Add a stock from a file you downloaded yourself -- no broker login needed. The first row must name the columns (Date, Open, High, Low, Close, Volume). Tabs or commas, dates like 01 Oct 2026 or 2026-10-01, and numbers like 1,180.1 all work, in any row order. By default the file is merged into the stock's existing history by date; tick Replace to discard the old history first. The stock starts with its first ~60 days showing and replays the rest as you advance the market. ALPHA, BETA, GAMMA and DELTA are simulated and can't be imported over.

    Use plain NSE trading symbols such as SBIN, WIPRO or M&M. Each stock is fetched and stored in your database like any other, so it appears in every dropdown and chart and replays on the market clock (it starts with its first ~60 days showing). By default a stock you already have is replaced by the broker's history; tick merge to keep anything the broker doesn't return. Your broker credentials stay in your local .env file and are never typed into this page.

    Monte Carlo: how much of this result was luck?

    A backtest is one path through history. Monte Carlo re-plays this run's closed trades thousands of times, resampled or in a new order, to show how wide the spread of outcomes could have been. It measures the luck inside this backtest; it does not predict the future.

    Optimiser

    Tries every combination of one or two of a strategy's settings on a training period, then runs the winner on a test period it has never seen. Picking the best of many settings on the same data you score them on is how overfitting happens; the test period is how you find out.

    Other settings stay at their defaults. Each combination is an ordinary backtest, run with your current Risk management and Trading costs settings, so a cell equals the same run on the Backtests page. Nothing is saved or traded.

    Choose a strategy, a stock and one or two settings to sweep, then press "Run optimisation". The default 70% / 30% split trains on the first 70% of the stored history and tests on the last 30%.

    Looking for the heatmaps? They belong to the Train / test split method, which shows every combination on one training and one test period. Walk-forward runs a separate optimisation per fold, so it shows the fold table instead.
